## Design Context

### Users

Chartbrew serves people who build and review business dashboards. Maya is a product or growth
operator who starts with a business question and must not need database, model, job, or scoring
terms. Benji is a technical owner who defines important metrics and needs clear setup, data quality,
and delivery controls. Both users need to understand what changed, which periods Chartbrew compared,
and what action is useful.

### Brand Personality

Clear, calm, and capable. The interface should help users trust the result without making the product
feel clinical or complex. Copy uses familiar business language and a direct, helpful tone.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use Chartbrew's existing refined product interface, HeroUI components, blue accent, light and dark
themes, and established spacing and type hierarchy. Prefer compact, scannable controls and progressive
disclosure. Do not add decorative cards, gradients, excessive helper text, or developer-facing
metadata. Icons support recognition but do not replace labels.

### Design Principles

- Lead with the business task and the exact result.
- Keep advanced calculation details out of the normal workflow.
- Use clear defaults and show only controls that affect a user decision.
- Name exact comparison periods and honest waiting states.
- Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, theme support, and responsive behavior.
